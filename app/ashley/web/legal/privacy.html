Privacy

Ashley, at $host · last changed 7 November 2026

Ashley is a private assistant that one person runs for their own household. It is not a commercial service and has no users outside that household. This page says what it keeps, where that goes, and how to take it back.

Who runs it

Ashley is run by $operator. Questions, or a request to delete anything below: $contact.

What it keeps

  • Conversations with household members, and what Ashley remembers from them.
  • Household data it is given access to: the home's devices and lists, reminders, and the services a member connects.
  • For each connected account (for example a Google account): the sign-in token, encrypted in the database under a key held only by the household's servers, and the list of mailboxes it reaches.
  • Mail is read from the provider when a member asks, or when a watch the member set up checks their inbox. Ashley does not store mail in its database. For Proton accounts, Proton Mail Bridge keeps a working copy on the household's servers while it runs, as Proton's own app does.
  • A record of each model call: which provider, how many tokens, and the cost. Not its content.

Where it runs

On the household's own servers, at two of its sites, in a database only those servers reach. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside the household.

Who else sees data

To answer a request, Ashley sends the text it needs to an AI model provider's API: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google (Gemini). Speech goes to ElevenLabs. When a member asks about their mail, the parts of messages needed to answer are sent the same way. Nothing else leaves the household's servers.

Google user data

A household member can connect a Google account so Ashley can read, search, label, draft and send their Gmail, using the gmail.modify scope. Ashley uses that access only for what the member asks for, or for a standing watch the member set up and agreed to. It sends mail only when the member asks, or when the member approves one Ashley held for approval. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and is not read by people except as needed to fix a problem the member reports.

Ashley's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Taking it back

  • Disconnect an account in Ashley's Connections room. That deletes its stored token.
  • Or remove Ashley's access from the Google account itself at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To have conversations or anything else deleted, email the address above.
