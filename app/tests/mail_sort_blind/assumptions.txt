Guesses of the blind sort suite (part 8). G1-G2 live in mail_sort_blind_support.py, G3-G5 and G9 in
mail_sort_blind_turn_support.py, G6-G8 in mail_sort_blind_arming_support.py; a reconciler changes the helper, not the tests.
The suite runs the real PostgresWatches store and the real `watches` job (database tests), the real watch_turn.inbound and
Landing/Outside with MailTools over an in-memory mail port (turn tests), and the real WatchTools/ReminderTools (arming).

G1  `brief` (str | None, default None), `standing` (bool, default False) and `grant` (frozenset[str], default empty) are
    fields of both ashley.watches.model.Arm and Watch, built by keyword; a test reads a grant as set(watch.grant).
    Rests on: "A watch gains `brief` ... and `standing`", "A watch gains `grant`: a set of label names".
G2  A standing watch's `hours` is the span it was armed for (expires_at at arming minus now). A read that succeeded
    (Baseline, Quiet or Woke) sets expires_at to now + hours; Failed moves nothing; arming again with other hours changes it.
    Rests on: "expires_at moves forward by `hours` on every successful read ... ends only when disarmed or after its source
    has failed for `hours`". Open: whether `hours` is a stored column or derived; the suite only sees the end it gives.
G3  watch_turn.inbound(wake_id, watch, wake, on_bench) carries the watch's grant to the turn however it likes; the turn's
    tool source is Landing(inbound, (conversation, turn), limits, tainted). A resumed turn is the same Inbound through
    channels.checkpoint dumps/loads (which must carry the grant) and Landing(..., tainted="watch").
    Rests on: "In that watch's woken turn, `Outside` runs a call the grant covers" and "What survives a failover".
G4  A label call is mail_label with the shipped arguments (mailbox, messages [{id, from, subject}], add, remove, folder,
    unread). The watch's mailbox is its `target`; "the message is in the watch's mailbox" is read as the call's `mailbox`
    argument being that mailbox. A folder move (trash, archive) is "moving/archiving" and held (Rejected list).
G5  A held call returns outside_config.load().held with no error and does not reach the port; a run call reaches the
    Mailbox port once with the call's ids and labels.
G6  `watch` takes `brief` (string), `standing` (boolean) and `grant` (list of strings) beside its other arguments; a
    malformed one is bad_arguments and arms and proposes nothing. A watch whose tools act is proposed (armed false) and its
    result names every granted label; arm_reminder of a later person turn arms it with brief, standing, grant and the tools
    as proposed. Rests on: "shown in the arming proposal and agreed with it, in a person's untainted turn like any
    agreement".
G7  A grant with no tool that acts is either not armed or armed with an empty grant.
G8  MailInbox.available(household_id, person_id) is True when ConnectionResources.usable gives the person a mailbox and False
    when it gives none. Rests on: part 7 left `available` False "until now".
G9  The brief reaches her in Inbound.note, never in Inbound.text.
    Rests on: "brief (what this watch's turn is for, as a reminder's brief is: people's data, never logged)".
