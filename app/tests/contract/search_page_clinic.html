Urgent care centers in Springfield

If you or a loved one needs timely and efficient treatment, Brightwater® urgent care can help. Our doctors and medical staff work extended hours to provide you care when you need it, with walk-in visits and short waits.

Our treatment options

Our doctors offer comprehensive care and are equipped to diagnose and treat a variety of acute conditions. Every clinic is staffed through the evening and on weekends, and each visit starts with a short check-in at the front desk.

Medical conditions we treat

When you visit our walk-in clinics, you’ll be seen by one of doctors and receive treatment the same day. To help us meet your medical needs, we offer treatment for a range of conditions, including:

  • Acid reflux
  • Allergies
  • Burns
  • Bruises
  • Chemical exposure
  • Childhood diseases
  • Common cold
  • Cuts and scrapes
  • Ear infections
  • Fevers
  • Flu
  • Headaches
  • Insect bites
  • Joint pain
  • Minor fractures
  • Nausea
  • Rashes
  • Sinus infections
  • Sore throat
  • Sprains
  • Urinary tract infections
  • and more

What to bring

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card and a list of the medicines you take. If you are visiting for a child, bring any record of their vaccines. Our staff will tell you what to expect at check-in, and most visits take less than an hour from the door to the exit.

Wait times change through the day. Early mornings and late evenings are usually the quietest, and the posted wait time at each clinic is updated every few minutes so you can plan the trip.
