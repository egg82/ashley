Equivalent or not testable from the design
E1   keeper   "writes the vault back before it handles anything else": a keeper that heartbeats from a second thread while a write-back is in flight; the design gives no observable (a slow write-back in flight is the same as a heartbeat sent after it), so no test pins the order.
E2   job      adopting a pod that exists in the record but not in Kubernetes: the design says "adopted as it is" on the record's word; the job cannot tell without a pod lookup the design does not require.
E3   record   `fingerprint` kept only when it changes: indistinguishable from keeping it every beat.
E4   routes   409 vs 401 for a retired epoch's token: the design says 409 for a non-current epoch; a token that belongs to an older epoch is accepted as 401, 403 or 409 by the tests.
E5   login    the app's relay timing: nothing in the design bounds a login step's duration.
E6   room     "the room shows the same" for syncing: part 4's room routes are not named; the record's `syncing` field is what is tested.

Killed by the part's own tests alone, the blind suite staying green: R22 (the record's `sync` taking a stale epoch is

K13 (a write-back that cannot reach the app ended Bridge only when the fence did, which the fence-bounded test cannot tell
from an immediate stop).
Variants: R3 drops the epoch from the write-back's statement and from `authorize` together (either alone is refused by the
other); R5 is +2, +0 and +length of the vault; R6 reads the generation, pauses, then writes unconditionally; S19 is the
namespace and the image; S20 the pod name and the site; K7 never takes the answer's generation and hard-codes 0; K14 drops
the Authorization header and drops the epoch; K15 logs the vault and the request headers; G2 logs the Authorization header
at a refused write-back and the token at a retired epoch.
Equivalent (no test can tell them apart, with the reason):

     per-epoch copy to answer from.
R18b begin_epoch leaves the old heartbeat in place: the build does it on purpose, so the new holder's wait is counted from
     the older pod's last heartbeat; the blind suite's `heartbeat_at` is None only on a fresh record.
R20  events route  sync_finished stored against the row's epoch at read time: an event is taken only where its epoch is the
     row's, so both values are the same.
L7   login         a signed-out caller reaches the keeper: the guard is the `Person` dependency every route of the app
