**Piece 3 rebuilt on the measured drawing (Tue, 21:43):** the rafters now cut longest first, the notches read left to right, and the overflow check matches the maker's. Version 2 also had a real bug: `trim(31)` came out negative half the time, so its cut lengths were wrong. Ran v3: it matches the maker's own cut list, and a 4 cm trim cut as if at full length matched **no board** in 200. So an unhandled short-offcut stretch most likely gives a blank, not a fake cut. Still open: whether the stock length rounds to a whole centimetre first (asked of the session).

**The plan itself:** A Birdhouse Basics site (example.test, dated 20 Oct) says the plan is free and open source, with its files on a code host. Still no repo link found (re-checked Tuesday evening, including the maker's video description, which look-up couldn't read). The maker's transcript confirms the cut list is a spreadsheet.

**The bug (from the maker's transcript, piece 16):** The spreadsheet's rounding rule has a carry: it adds a millimetre when the remainder passes the saw kerf and goes negative. The sheet checks for a negative number, but a spreadsheet cell never overflows, so the carry never fires and the sheet can accept a length the saw would have trimmed. He puts it at about 1 in 100 cuts, so with ~15 cuts in the video it's unlikely to have mattered. His independent rerun found cuts at the same places the sheet did.

**Our piece 3 already handles this:** its `round_cut` compares against `1 << 4` rather than `< 0`, which is the correct stand-in for the saw's carry. Not yet tested against a length that actually triggers a carry.

**Points the transcript confirms for piece 3:** cuts only with the kerf at 0; at full length they fall on boards divisible by 7 (width × 3 + 1 with width 2); the count is the shop count, from the first board; short offcuts change the cuts (the notch drawing from the same list, as piece 3 assumed).

**Short offcuts are handled (piece 19, 6:04):** a cut code takes an optional **H** at two boards or less with notches marked, for the extra notch cuts. Remaining risk: the automated scan missing an H, which piece 3's run says gives no match rather than a false one.

**The maker's drawing:** checked against a v1.2 print on Tuesday; see the drawing note for what it found. The questions it answered: To confirm for the roof pitch (rafter cut, ridge height):
- Is the saw angle reset for each rafter from the board count × 7?
- Is the notch cut per board, and what draws from that list first at short lengths?
